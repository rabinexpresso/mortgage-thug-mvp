📖 How to Play
कसरी खेल्ने?
1
Add all players in clockwise order — minimum 3 players. Drag ☰ to reorder. With 6+ players, choose 1 or 2 ठगs at the bottom of this page.
2
Choose one or more of the 23 categories. A secret word is randomly picked from the combined pool. Some categories (🔞) are password protected.
3
Pass the phone to each player. Drag the pink card to reveal your role. The धितो ठग (Mortgage Thug) sees only their role — not the secret word. With 6+ players, there may be 2 ठगs who don't know each other!
4
The first player is randomly selected. Go clockwise around the circle — each player says one vague word clue. The ठग tries to figure out the secret word from the clues.
5
Discuss & vote for who you think is the धितो ठग. The player with the most votes is caught.
6
If caught, the ठग gets one chance to guess the secret word. Guess correctly = ठग still wins!
7
If the ठग is NOT caught, the ठग wins. If caught and wrong guess, the team wins!
8
The first non-ठग player to reveal their role can tap 🔄 Reset Word to pick a different secret word before passing the phone.

🏆 How to win

ठग wins by not getting caught OR by guessing the secret word after being caught.
Team wins by correctly identifying and catching the ठग.
